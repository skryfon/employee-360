## 1. Project Overview

Employee360 is a centralised employee platform that brings together essential company information, policies, benefits, career development, learning opportunities, compensation and taxation details, and performance-related resources in one convenient place.

The first phase of the system will focus on developing a **Holiday Calendar Management System**, allowing administrators to configure and manage company holidays while enabling employees to view the applicable holiday calendar after logging in using their official company email.

The system will consist of two primary interfaces:

1. **Admin Application** – For configuring and managing holidays.
2. **Employee Application** – For employees to securely log in and view the company holiday calendar.

---

## 2. Objectives

The primary objectives of Phase 1 are:

- Provide a centralised holiday calendar for the organisation.
- Allow authorised administrators to create, edit, and delete holidays.
- Allow employees to view the official holiday calendar using their company email.
- Eliminate dependency on manually circulated holiday lists.
- Ensure employees always have access to the latest approved holiday information.

---

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In Scope

Phase 1 will include:

- Employee authentication using company email.
- Admin authentication and authorisation.
- Holiday calendar configuration.
- Adding new holidays.
- Editing existing holidays.
- Removing holidays.
- Viewing holidays by employees.
- Calendar/list-based holiday display.
- Holiday categorisation.
- Year-wise holiday calendars.
- Basic audit information for administrative changes.

---

## 4. Key Principles

- **Open Source** – Employee360 will be developed as an open-source initiative, enabling organisations to use, customise, and contribute to the platform.
- **Multi-Tenant** – The platform will support multiple organisations, with each company operating within its own secure and isolated tenant.
- **Self-Hostable** – Organisations can deploy and host Employee360 within their own infrastructure, providing greater control over data, security, and deployment.
- **Headless Architecture** – The backend will follow a headless architecture, exposing APIs that can be consumed by web, mobile, or other client applications independently of the presentation layer.
